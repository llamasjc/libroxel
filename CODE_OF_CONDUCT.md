# Code of Conduct

PlayLibre is a place for people of every background and experience level to
build games together. We want everyone who takes part, whether in issues,
pull requests, chat, or community events, to feel welcome and safe.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and with people
  whose first language isn't English.
- Give and receive feedback gracefully. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask questions before jumping to conclusions.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to anyone affected, and learn
  from them.
- Remember that many community members are young. Keep everything suitable
  for a general audience.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking or threats of any kind.
- Insults, slurs, or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity, including
  age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of
  experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.
- Sexual language or imagery, and any sexual attention toward others.
- Sharing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained off-topic derailing.
- Encouraging or helping anyone else to do any of the above.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the
project in public, for example by posting from an official account.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it to the
maintainers at **conduct@playlibre.net** <!-- TODO: replace with a real
contact address before publishing --> or by messaging a maintainer
privately. Reports are handled confidentially, and we will not retaliate
against anyone for reporting in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review every report and respond in a way they judge
appropriate and proportionate. Depending on the situation, that can be:

1. **A private note** explaining what was inappropriate and why.
2. **A warning**, with clear consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **A temporary ban** from interacting with the project.
4. **A permanent ban** for serious or repeated violations.

Maintainers who don't follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
the same consequences.
