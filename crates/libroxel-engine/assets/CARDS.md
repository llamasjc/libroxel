# Cards

(https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/aisleriot, `cards/anglo.svg`),
(C) 2007 Aike Reyer and (C) 2024 Vincent Bermel, cut into one PNG per
card. `cards/baize.jpg` is AisleRiot's table cloth (`src/data/baize.png`)
repeated. Both are under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or
later (`GPL-3.0.txt`).
