# Chess pieces

`chess-*.png` are the "cburnett" pieces Lichess uses, taken from
https://github.com/lichess-org/lila (`public/piece/cburnett`) and turned
into 128x128 PNGs. They were drawn by Colin M.L. Burnett
(https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User:Cburnett).

Lichess lists them under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or
later. The license text is in `CHESS-PIECES-GPL-2.0.txt` beside this file.
The original SVG files are at the address above.

Only these twelve pictures are under the GPL; the rest of Libroxel keeps
its MIT or Apache 2.0 license.
