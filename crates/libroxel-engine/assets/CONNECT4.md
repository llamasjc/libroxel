# Connect 4

`connect4/c4-*.png` are the "Stars and Rings" tiles from GNOME
Four-in-a-row (https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/four-in-a-row,
`data/themes/tileset_50x50_faenza-gnect-icon.svg`), based on the Faenza
icon theme by Tiheum, cut into one PNG per tile. They are under the GNU
General Public License, version 3 or later (`GPL-3.0.txt`).
