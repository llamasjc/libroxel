# Sounds

`sounds/lichess/` holds Lichess's sfx, piano, nes and futuristic sound sets,
from https://github.com/lichess-org/lila (`public/sound`), made by
Enigmahack (https://github.com/Enigmahack). The files are renamed and
otherwise unchanged.

`sounds/lidraughts/` holds the piano set as Lidraughts ships it, from
https://github.com/RoepStoep/lidraughts (`public/sound/piano`), based on
the same Lichess set.

Both are under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later
(`AGPL-3.0.txt` beside this file).

`sounds/four-in-a-row/` is from GNOME Four-in-a-row
(https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/four-in-a-row, `data/sounds`) and
`sounds/aisleriot/` from GNOME AisleRiot
(https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/aisleriot, `data/sounds`). Both are under
the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later (`GPL-3.0.txt`).

Only these files are under those licenses; the rest of Libroxel keeps its
MIT or Apache 2.0 license.
